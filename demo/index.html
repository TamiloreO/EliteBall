Team sheets assessed values

      Model

      Every player carries ability values from 1 to 99 plus a condition line: fitness, sharpness, morale and the distraction a rumour adds. Possessions resolve into passes, carries and shots at rates set by those values and by team tactics, and each shot is worth an expected-goals figure. The same seed always plays the same match.

      Squads are invented, and the match is a stylised model rather than a calibrated forecast. Load your own licensed data locally; nothing third-party ships in this repository.
