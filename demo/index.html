      The model

      Every player carries ability values from 1 to 99 plus a condition line: fitness, sharpness, morale and the distraction a rumour adds. Possessions resolve into passes, carries and shots at rates set by those values and by team tactics, and every shot is worth an expected-goals figure. The same seed always plays the same match.

      Squads are invented and shown by jersey number and initials. The match is a stylised model, not a calibrated forecast, and no third-party data ships in this repository.
